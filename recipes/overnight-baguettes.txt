Overnight Baguettes (4)

Ingredients
500 g flour
350 g water
10 g salt (1¾ tsp)
0.8 g instant yeast (¼ tsp)

Method
1. Mix flour and water until no dry flour remains. Cover, rest 20 min.
2. Sprinkle yeast and salt on top and work them in with a wet hand. Rubaud mix 2–3 min. Rest 15 min, then Rubaud mix 2–3 min more.
3. 1 h at room temp, stretch and fold every 20 min (3 folds).
4. Leave at room temp ~30 min, until slightly puffy with a few bubbles.
5. Cover, fridge ~21 h.
6. Divide cold into 4 (~215 g). Pre-shape into loose logs. Cover, rest ~1 h, until puffier and relaxed.
7. Shape into demi-baguettes. Proof in the couche ~1 h, until clearly puffed.
8. Meanwhile preheat to 250°C with steam setup.
9. Score. Bake 250°C, steam for the first 10 min, then without. About 20 min total, until golden.
