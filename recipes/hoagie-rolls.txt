Hoagie rolls (about 10)

Ingredients
600 g flour (strong)
245 g water, lukewarm
130 g milk, lukewarm
35 g vegetable oil
20 g dark brown sugar
10 g fine salt (1¾ tsp)
5 g instant yeast (1½ tsp)

Method
1. Mix the water, milk and oil, then stir in the yeast.
2. Mix the flour, salt and sugar in a large bowl, pour in the liquids, and stir 4 min. Rest 5 min, stir 2 min more, then knead lightly in the bowl. It should be smooth and tacky, not sticky.
3. Oiled container, covered, fridge overnight or up to 4 days.
4. Take out 2 h before baking. Divide into 10 (~105 g) and shape into torpedoes while still cold.
5. Rise 1 h at room temp on parchment.
6. Oven to 220°C. Slash the rolls and let them rise 15 min more.
7. Put them in and drop the oven to 200°C right away. Bake 20–25 min, rotating halfway. Steam is optional.
