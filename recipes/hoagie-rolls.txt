Hoagie rolls (about 10)

Dough
600 g Caputo red
245 g water, lukewarm
130 g milk, lukewarm
35 g vegetable oil (2½ tbsp)
20 g dark brown sugar (1½ tbsp)
10 g fine salt (1¾ tsp)
5 g instant yeast (1½ tsp)

Mix the water, milk and oil, then stir in the yeast.
Mix the flour, salt and sugar in a large bowl, pour in the liquids, and stir 4 min. Rest 5 min, stir 2 min more, then knead lightly in the bowl. It should be smooth and tacky, not sticky.
Oiled container, covered, fridge overnight or up to 4 days.
Take out 2 h before baking. Divide into 10 (~105 g) and shape into torpedoes while still cold.
Rise 1 h at room temp on parchment.
Oven to 220°C. Slash the rolls and let them rise 15 min more.
Put them in and drop the oven to 200°C right away. Bake 20–25 min, rotating halfway. Steam is optional.
