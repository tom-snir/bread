Pretzel rolls (8) or pretzels (12)

Ingredients
Dough:
600 g flour (strong)
340 g water, room temp
40 g soft butter
18 g dark brown sugar (1½ tbsp)
13 g fine salt (2 tsp)
5 g instant yeast (1½ tsp)

Bath (stainless pot):
2000 g water
100 g baking soda

Topping:
Coarse salt

Method
1. Mix and knead until smooth and firm.
2. Bulk at room temp, covered, until puffy but not doubled.
3. Deflate, fridge covered 4–5 h.
4. Divide: 8 for rolls (~125 g) or 12 for pretzels (~85 g). Shape. For pretzels, roll the ropes in two passes with a rest in between.
5. Proof covered at room temp until about 1.5×, around 30 min.
6. Dip 30 sec per side, slash, salt.
7. Bake. Rolls: 200°C, lowest rack, about 21 min. Pretzels: 230°C, 10–15 min until golden-brown.
