Pretzel rolls (8) or pretzels (12)

600 g Caputo red
340 g water, room temp
40 g soft butter (3 tbsp)
18 g dark brown sugar (1½ tbsp)
13 g fine salt (2 tsp)
5 g instant yeast (1½ tsp)

Bath: 2 L water + 100 g baking soda (½ cup), stainless pot
Coarse salt for topping

Mix and knead until smooth and firm.
Bulk at room temp, covered, until puffy but not doubled.
Deflate, fridge covered 4–5 h.
Divide: 8 for rolls (~125 g) or 12 for pretzels (~85 g). Shape. For pretzels, roll the ropes in two passes with a rest in between.
Proof covered at room temp until about 1.5×, around 30 min.
Dip 30 sec per side, slash, salt.
Bake. Rolls: 200°C, lowest rack, about 21 min. Pretzels: 230°C, 10–15 min until golden-brown.
