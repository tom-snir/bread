Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients
227 g unsalted butter
35 g whole milk, cold
150 g dark brown sugar
150 g granulated sugar
2 eggs (about 100 g out of shell)
15 g vanilla extract (1 tbsp)
260 g flour
6 g baking soda (1 tsp)
6 g fine salt (1 tsp)
140 g bittersweet chocolate, half chopped, half in chunks
140 g milk chocolate, half chopped, half in chunks

Method
1. Brown the butter: melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium-low heat, scraping the bottom constantly, until it foams and the solids turn deep golden and smell nutty (about 5-7 min).
2. Off the heat, pour in the milk (it will sputter) and whisk until smooth. Scrape everything, including the brown bits, into a bowl.
3. Cool to roughly room temperature, stirring now and then. An ice bath speeds this up, but stop once it's just turning opaque. Don't let it set solid.
4. Add both sugars and whisk until smooth and thick.
5. Whisk in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla, until glossy.
6. Whisk flour, baking soda and salt together, then fold into the wet mixture with a spatula until no dry flour remains. The dough should look a little loose.
7. Fold in both chocolates.
8. Scoop into about 18 balls of roughly 56 g. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours, up to 48.
9. Heat oven to 180°C. Bake 6 at a time on a parchment-lined sheet, spaced well apart, rotating the sheet halfway, until edges are golden brown and centers still look soft (18-20 min). Keep the rest of the dough in the fridge between batches.
10. Rest on the sheet 5 minutes, then move to a rack to cool.
