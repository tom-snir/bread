The Overnight Challah

Ingredients
700 g flour (strong)
280 g water, plus 20 g on standby
2 large eggs
65 g honey
5 g instant yeast (1½ tsp)
14 g fine salt (2½ tsp)
60 g extra virgin olive oil
1 egg, beaten, for egg wash

Method
1. The Shaggy Mix: Whisk the 280 g of water, eggs, honey, and yeast together in a large bowl. Dump in the flour and salt, and mix it by hand until it forms a shaggy, dry mass.
2. The Hydration Rest: Cover the bowl and walk away for 20 minutes to let the flour hydrate on its own.
3. Hand Knead: Turn it out onto the counter and knead for 5–8 minutes. If it feels excessively stiff and dry, work in that extra standby water a splash at a time.
4. Incorporate the Oil: Pour the oil over the dough and knead it in. It will tear and look messy at first; keep pushing and folding until it comes back together into a smooth, cohesive, and slightly tacky ball (about 5 more minutes).
5. The Kickstart: Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl. Let it sit uncovered at room temperature for 30 to 45 minutes to get the yeast active.
6. The Retard: Cover the bowl tightly with plastic wrap to prevent a skin from forming, and place it in the fridge for 12 to 24 hours.
7. Cold Shape and First Wash: Take the dough out of the fridge, punch it down, divide, and braid your loaves immediately while the dough is still cold and stiff. Place on a lined baking sheet and brush with your first coat of egg wash.
8. Extended Second Proof: Cover loosely and let them rise at room temperature. Because the dough is cold, this will take 2 to 3 hours. It is ready when puffy and a gentle poke leaves an indentation that slowly springs back.
9. Second Wash and Bake: Brush with a second coat of egg wash. Bake at 175°C for 30–35 minutes, until deep mahogany brown and the internal temperature hits 90°C. (Note: If baking in a loaf pan, drop the oven to 165°C, bake for 40–45 minutes, and slide an empty baking sheet on the rack directly above the bread halfway through the bake to prevent the top from scorching).
