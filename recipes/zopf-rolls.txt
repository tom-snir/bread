Zopf rolls (overnight)

Ingredients
500 g Pivetti challah flour
300 g cold milk (+ up to 10 g if stiff)
60 g soft butter
10 g fine salt
4 g sugar (1 tsp)
1 scant tsp instant yeast
1 egg + splash of milk, for egg wash

Method
Mix the flour, salt, sugar and yeast. Add the cold milk and soft butter and knead about 10 min until smooth.
Oiled container, covered, into the fridge for about 20 h.
Next day, divide while cold: 8 (~110 g) or 12 (~73 g).
Pre-shape into balls, cover, rest 15 min.
Shape and place on parchment.
Proof covered until puffy, 1–2 h.
Oven to 200°C.
Brush with egg wash, wait 5–10 min, brush again.
Bake in the lower half of the oven until deep golden brown: about 20 min for 8, 15–20 min for 12.
