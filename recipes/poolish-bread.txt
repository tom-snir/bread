70% Hydration Poolish Bread
Yield: 2 loaves (~690g each)

POOLISH
- 200g bread flour
- 200g water
- Scant 1/8 tsp instant yeast

FINAL DOUGH
- All of the poolish
- 600g bread flour
- 360g water
- 16g salt
- 1/4 tsp instant yeast

BAKER'S %
Water 70% / Salt 2% / Poolish flour 25%

STEPS
1. Poolish: Mix and leave at room temperature for 12 hours.
2. Mix: Combine with final dough ingredients. Rest 20 min.
3. Fold: 2-3 sets of folds, 20 min apart.
4. Bulk: Until ~50% rise from initial mix.
5. Shape: Divide in 2. Pre-shape, rest 15 min, final shape into floured bannetons.
6. Cold retard: 14 up to 30 hours in the fridge.
7. Bake: Preheat to 250C. Bake from the fridge with steam for 15 min, then vent, drop to 210C, and bake 25-30 min more until deep brown.
