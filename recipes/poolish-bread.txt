Poolish Bread

Makes 2 loaves

Ingredients
Poolish:
200 g flour (strong)
200 g water
0.3 g instant yeast (scant ⅛ tsp)

Final dough:
All of the poolish
600 g flour (strong)
360 g water
16 g salt (2¾ tsp)
0.8 g instant yeast (¼ tsp)

Baker's %: water 70%, salt 2%, poolish flour 25%

Method
1. Poolish: Mix and leave at room temperature for 12 hours.
2. Mix: Combine with final dough ingredients. Rest 20 min.
3. Fold: 2-3 sets of folds, 20 min apart.
4. Bulk: Until ~50% rise from initial mix.
5. Shape: Divide in 2 (~690 g each). Pre-shape, rest 15 min, final shape into floured bannetons.
6. Cold retard: 14 up to 30 hours in the fridge.
7. Bake: Preheat to 250°C. Bake from the fridge with steam for 15 min, then vent, drop to 210°C, and bake 25-30 min more until deep brown.
