Bagels

Makes 16 bagels

Ingredients
1200 g flour (high-gluten)
648 g water
5 g instant yeast (1½ tsp)
30 g fine salt
36 g neutral oil
60 g dark brown sugar
6 g diastatic malt powder (2 tsp)
42 g honey, for the boil

Method
1. Whisk flour, yeast, salt, brown sugar, and malt powder. Add water and oil; mix to a shaggy mass.
2. Knead by hand 10–15 min to a smooth windowpane. If stubborn, rest 10 min mid-knead and finish.
3. Rest dough covered at room temperature for 45–60 minutes, or until it has increased in size by about 50%.
4. Divide into 16 pieces (~124 g each). Roll into snakes, wrap around your hand, and seal. Place on oil-misted parchment.
5. Cover and refrigerate immediately for 16–24 hours.
6. Preheat oven to 240°C. Bring a wide pot of water to a boil and stir in the honey.
7. Boil bagels straight from the fridge for 10–15 seconds per side.
8. Transfer to a baking sheet and bake at 240°C for about 15 minutes until colored. Cool on a wire rack.
