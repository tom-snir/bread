Tom's Bagels (Makes 16)

Ingredients:

    1200g Bread/high-gluten flour

    648g Water

    5g Instant yeast (1.5 tsp)

    30g Salt (5 tsp fine)

    36g Neutral oil (2.5 tbsp)

    60g Dark brown sugar (4 tbsp packed)

    6g Diastatic malt powder (2 tsp)

    2 tbsp Honey (for the boil)

Instructions:

    Whisk flour, yeast, salt, brown sugar, and malt powder. Add water and oil; mix to a shaggy mass.

    Knead by hand 10–15 min to a smooth windowpane. If stubborn, rest 10 min mid-knead and finish.

    Rest dough covered at room temperature for 45–60 minutes, or until it has increased in size by about 50%.

    Divide into 16 pieces (~124g each). Roll into snakes, wrap around your hand, and seal. Place on oil-misted parchment.

    Cover and refrigerate immediately for 16–24 hours.

    Preheat oven to 240°C. Bring a wide pot of water to a boil and stir in the honey.

    Boil bagels straight from the fridge for 10–15 seconds per side.

    Transfer to a baking sheet and bake at 240°C for about 15 minutes until colored. Cool on a wire rack.
